Our approach

What does eco-friendly cleaning mean?

Eco-friendly cleaning is not simply about replacing every cleaning product with a product labelled "green".

A responsible cleaning approach considers how products are selected, how much is used, what cleaning materials are required and how the cleaning process can be completed efficiently.

We assess the requirements of each property and select appropriate cleaning methods based on the surfaces, environment and level of cleaning required.

Our practical green cleaning principles

  • ✓ Use environmentally conscious products where appropriate
  • ✓ Avoid unnecessary use of cleaning chemicals
  • ✓ Use cleaning products according to their intended purpose
  • ✓ Make practical use of reusable cleaning materials
  • ✓ Focus on efficient cleaning processes
  • ✓ Tailor cleaning methods to the property
