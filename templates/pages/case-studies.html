Corporate Office Deep Clean & Maintenance Program

A high-traffic professional workplace required a deeper clean and a more consistent maintenance routine.

The challenge

Carpet stains, busy reception areas, dusty conference rooms and hygiene-sensitive spaces were becoming increasingly difficult to manage with the existing cleaning routine.

Our approach

We assessed high-traffic zones and surface types, completed detailed deep cleaning, used HEPA filtration, carpet steam cleaning, glass cleaning and targeted sanitisation, then established an ongoing maintenance schedule.
