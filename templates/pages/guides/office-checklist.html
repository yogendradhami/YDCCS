PROFESSIONAL ADVICE

Office Cleaning Tips

Declutter First

Remove unnecessary items before cleaning so surfaces and floor areas are easier to access.

Work Top to Bottom

Start with higher surfaces and work down towards the floor to reduce repeated cleaning.

Use the Right Products

Select cleaning products that are suitable for the specific surface and always follow manufacturer instructions.

Prioritise Touchpoints

Give additional attention to handles, switches, shared equipment and other frequently touched areas.

Maintain Equipment

Clean and maintain cleaning equipment regularly so it continues to perform effectively.

Stay Consistent

A consistent cleaning routine helps prevent buildup and maintains a professional workplace environment.
