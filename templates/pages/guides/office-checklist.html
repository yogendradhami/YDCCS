Professional Office Cleaning Tips

  • Declutter first: Remove unnecessary items before cleaning so surfaces and hard-to-reach areas can be accessed properly.
  • Work from top to bottom: Start with higher surfaces and finish with floors to avoid re-contaminating cleaned areas.
  • Use the right cleaning products: Different surfaces require different cleaning products and methods. Follow manufacturer instructions where applicable.
  • Prioritise high-touch areas: Pay extra attention to door handles, switches, shared equipment and other frequently touched surfaces.
  • Maintain cleaning equipment: Clean and maintain vacuum cleaners, mops and other equipment regularly for better results.
  • Keep a consistent schedule: Regular cleaning helps prevent dirt and buildup while maintaining a professional workplace environment.
