How to use this cleaning schedule

Maintaining a consistently clean workplace is easier when cleaning responsibilities are organised around a clear schedule. This guide separates routine cleaning into daily, weekly, monthly, quarterly and annual tasks.

Use the checklist for internal cleaning management, assign responsibilities to team members, or use it as a starting point when planning a professional commercial cleaning program.

Tips for Maintaining a Clean Workplace

  • Be consistent: Regular cleaning is generally easier and more effective than allowing dirt and buildup to accumulate.
  • Prioritise high-touch areas: Pay particular attention to handles, switches, shared equipment and other frequently touched surfaces.
  • Match frequency to usage: High-traffic facilities may require more frequent cleaning than smaller or lower-traffic workplaces.
  • Record completed work: Use checklists to track tasks and identify recurring issues.
  • Schedule specialist cleaning: Carpets, windows and other specialist surfaces may require professional equipment and techniques.
