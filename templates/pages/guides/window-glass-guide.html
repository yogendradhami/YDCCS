Clean windows can make a property look brighter, fresher and more professional. The right equipment, cleaning solution and technique can help remove dust, fingerprints, water spots and everyday grime without leaving streaks.

Use this guide as a practical checklist for routine window cleaning. For high windows, difficult access or commercial properties, professional equipment and appropriate safety procedures may be required.

05

Professional Tips for Streak-Free Windows

Small changes in technique can make a noticeable difference to the final result.

Timing

Avoid strong direct sunlight where possible because cleaning solution can dry too quickly.

Keep the Squeegee Clean

Wipe the blade regularly to reduce streaks and water marks.

Use Suitable Water

Distilled water can be useful where hard water or mineral deposits are causing spotting.

Work Methodically

Clean one section at a time and work from top to bottom.

Inspect Before Finishing

Check the glass from multiple angles and correct any remaining streaks or marks.
