Before You Begin

Post-construction cleaning should generally be completed after major construction work has finished, including final painting, fixture installation and flooring or carpet installation.

Construction sites can contain fine dust, adhesive residue, protective films and other debris that require detailed cleaning. Always make sure the property is safe and suitable for cleaning before work begins.

Pro Tips

Post-Construction Cleaning Tips

  • Hire professionals: Post-construction cleaning can be intensive and may require specialist equipment, products and techniques.
  • Choose the right timing: Schedule cleaning after major construction work is complete and before final inspection or handover.
  • Prioritise safety: Confirm that the property is safe to enter and clean before beginning work.
  • Use suitable products: Different surfaces require different cleaning products. Always follow manufacturer recommendations.
  • Document the finished work: Photos can help record the condition of the property after cleaning.
