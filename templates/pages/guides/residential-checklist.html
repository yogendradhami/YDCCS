Helpful Advice

Home Cleaning Tips

A few simple habits can make regular household cleaning faster, easier and more effective.

01

Declutter First

Remove unnecessary items before cleaning so that surfaces and floors are easier to access.

02

Work Top to Bottom

Start with higher surfaces and work downwards to avoid putting dust back onto areas you have already cleaned.

03

Use a Timer

Short, focused cleaning sessions can make routine household cleaning easier to manage.

04

Keep Supplies Handy

Keep your commonly used cleaning products and equipment together so you can start quickly.

05

Use the Right Products

Choose cleaning products that are suitable for the specific surface and always follow manufacturer instructions.
