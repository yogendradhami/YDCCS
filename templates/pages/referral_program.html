Frequently asked questions

Referral program FAQs

Who can I refer?

You can refer friends, family members, neighbours, homeowners, tenants, landlords, property managers and local businesses that may require cleaning services.

How does the referred customer identify my referral?

Ask the person you refer to mention your name when they contact YD Commercial Cleaning or request their quote. This helps us identify the referral correctly.

When do I receive my reward?

Referral rewards are generally associated with a qualifying completed booking. Eligibility may depend on the service, booking status and applicable referral conditions.

Can I refer more than one person?

Yes. You can recommend our services to multiple people. Each referral is assessed according to the applicable referral program conditions.

Can businesses participate?

Yes. Local businesses, property professionals and other organisations can recommend YD Commercial Cleaning to suitable customers or business contacts.

Is there a cash reward?

Referral rewards may be provided as an applicable discount, service credit or another approved reward. Please contact us for the current referral offer and eligibility requirements.

Referral conditions

A few important things to know.

Referral rewards are subject to eligibility and the current YD Commercial Cleaning referral offer. A referral must be identifiable before or during the referred customer's booking process.

  • The referred customer must be a new or otherwise eligible customer where required by the current offer.
  • A referral reward may only apply after an eligible booking has been completed.
  • Duplicate, fraudulent or self-referrals may not qualify.
  • Referral rewards cannot necessarily be combined with other promotional offers.
  • Referral rewards, eligibility requirements and program conditions may change from time to time.

For the latest referral reward and eligibility requirements, please contact YD Commercial Cleaning before making a referral.
