PRICING FAQ

Questions before you book?

Here are some of the common pricing questions. If you're unsure, contact us and we'll help.

Are the prices on this page final?

No. They are indicative starting prices. Final pricing can depend on property size, condition, scope, frequency, access and additional requirements.

How is commercial cleaning priced?

Commercial cleaning is custom quoted around the premises, cleaning scope, frequency, operating hours and other site requirements.

Can I request a customised cleaning service?

Yes. Tell us what you need cleaned and we can discuss the appropriate scope and service arrangement.

Does the calculator give me a guaranteed price?

No. The calculator is an estimate based on the starting prices shown on this page. Your confirmed quote may differ after your requirements are reviewed.

Are there additional charges?

If the actual condition or scope differs significantly from what was agreed, additional charges may apply. We aim to clarify requirements and pricing before service.
