Before You Book

If you are unsure about any part of your booking, our team is happy to discuss your requirements before the appointment is confirmed.

Important Information

Booking terms explained

Please review the following information before confirming your cleaning appointment.

✓

1. Booking confirmation

A cleaning request is not considered confirmed until the required customer information has been received and the scheduled service time has been agreed.

Providing accurate information helps us prepare the appropriate service and allocate enough time for your requirements.

i

2. Customer information

Please provide accurate information about the property and cleaning requirements when requesting a booking.

Important details may include the property type, approximate size, number of rooms, condition of the property and any areas requiring special attention.

$

3. Pricing & scope of work

Cleaning prices depend on factors such as property size, service type, cleaning condition and the scope of work requested.

If the actual condition or requested work is significantly different from the information provided when the booking was arranged, additional work or charges may apply.

⌂

4. Property access

Customers are responsible for providing safe and reasonable access to the property at the agreed appointment time.

Please provide any access instructions, keys, entry codes or other information required for our team to enter safely.

+

5. Preparing for your cleaning

Customers can help make the service more efficient by removing personal valuables and providing clear access to areas that need cleaning.

Please let us know in advance about fragile items, restricted areas, pets or other special requirements.

↻

6. Cancellations & rescheduling

If you need to cancel or reschedule your appointment, please contact us as soon as possible.

Reasonable notice allows our team to reorganise the schedule and offer the appointment time to another customer. Additional charges may apply where circumstances require additional time or work.

$

7. Payments

Payment arrangements depend on the agreed service, quotation and customer arrangement.

Payment details and any applicable requirements will be communicated before or as part of confirming the service.

+

8. Additional work

Some properties may require additional cleaning beyond the original scope discussed during booking.

Where additional work is required, we will discuss the situation with the customer and explain any applicable additional charges where practical.

★

9. Special requirements

Please tell us before the appointment if your property has special cleaning requirements, restricted areas or other circumstances that may affect the service.

This helps our team prepare appropriately and provide a safer, more efficient service.

Frequently Asked Questions

Booking questions answered

If you cannot find the answer you need, contact our team and we will be happy to help.

How is my booking confirmed? +

Your booking is confirmed once we receive the required customer details and confirm the scheduled service time with you.

How is the cleaning price determined? +

Pricing depends on factors such as property size, service type, condition and the scope of work requested.

Can I request additional cleaning? +

Yes. If you require additional areas or services, please discuss them with us. Additional work may require additional charges depending on the scope.

What happens if I need to reschedule? +

Please contact us as soon as possible. Reasonable notice helps us rearrange the schedule and manage your booking efficiently.

Do I need to provide access? +

Yes. Customers are responsible for providing safe access to the property at the agreed appointment time.

What if nobody is home? +

Please arrange an agreed access method with our team before the appointment if you will not be present.

Should I tell you about pets? +

Yes. Please let us know about pets or other circumstances that may affect safe access or the cleaning service.

How are payments handled? +

Payment arrangements depend on the agreed quotation and service. Payment details are communicated as part of the booking arrangement.

What happens if the property needs more work? +

If the actual condition or requested scope is significantly different from what was discussed, additional work may be required. We will discuss this with you where practical.

Who can I contact if I have questions? +

Contact YD Commercial Cleaning Services on 0430 049 865 or use our online contact form. We are happy to discuss your cleaning requirements before confirming your booking.
