Before your booking

What customers should know

A successful cleaning appointment starts with clear communication between the customer and our team.

01

Service scope

Cleaning requirements can vary depending on the property, service type, condition and areas that need attention. Please provide accurate information when requesting a service.

02

Booking information

Please provide accurate contact details and relevant information about the property when making a booking or requesting a quote.

03

Property access

Where access arrangements are required, customers should make sure our cleaning team can safely access the agreed areas at the scheduled time.

04

Communication

If anything changes before your appointment, please contact us as soon as possible so appropriate arrangements can be discussed.

05

Payments & invoices

Payment and invoicing arrangements may depend on the service and booking. Refer to the applicable booking terms for more information.

Questions about our policies
