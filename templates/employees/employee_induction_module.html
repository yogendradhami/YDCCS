ON THE JOB

How this applies to your work

01

Arrive prepared

Make sure you have the required uniform, equipment and information before starting work.

02

Follow the procedure

Do not skip required steps simply because a task appears familiar or straightforward.

03

Check your work

Take a moment to inspect the result and correct anything that does not meet the expected standard.

04

Communicate early

Report hazards, incidents, damaged equipment, access issues or other concerns promptly.

THE YD STANDARD

Clean like someone is going to inspect it.

Our standard is not simply to finish a task. It is to deliver a clean, safe and professional result that we would be confident putting our name behind.

SAFETY REMINDER

Never take shortcuts where safety is concerned.

If you are unsure about a chemical, piece of equipment, hazard, access condition or work procedure, stop and speak with your supervisor before proceeding.
